Customer Friction Governance: Defense-Only Optimization Symmetric Economic Tradeoffs

Traditional fraud systems maximize blocks, destroying customer lifetime value through false positives. The AI Risk Manager explicitly penalizes customer friction in its utility function $V(a) = \text{Recovery} - \text{Expected Loss} - \text{Friction Cost}(a) - \text{Op Cost}(a)$, guaranteeing that intrusive interventions (OTP, Store Credit, Human Review) are deployed only when expected fraud savings mathematically exceed the customer friction cost.
